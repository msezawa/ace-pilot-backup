Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

What this app accesses

ACE Pilot Gmail requests only the Gmail scope gmail.readonly. It reads the owner's own messages and never sends, deletes, labels, moves, or changes any email.

How the data is used

Messages are read on the owner's computer and kept there, to compare the owner's drafts with what was actually sent and to propose writing rules. A rule is used only after the owner approves it. There is no server operated by the developer; nothing is uploaded to one.

When the owner drafts an email with an AI assistant that the owner uses, the approved writing rules may be shown to that assistant for that draft only. Email contents are not sent to any external AI service unless the owner has explicitly allowed it for a specific purpose.

Limited Use

ACE Pilot Gmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or used by any third party. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Who uses it

Only the owner of this app uses it. It is not offered to the public.

Keeping and removing data

Data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own encrypted backups. The owner can exclude messages from learning or delete them at any time. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
